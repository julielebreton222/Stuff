Wizard Scorekeeper

Round

Type each player's score for the round (tap ± for a minus). Tick Zero if they bid and made zero. In the final rounds a Blind 0 button appears for blind zeroes. Streak bonuses and final-round multipliers are added for you.

Every round

Each cell: points scored that round, with the running total underneath. Cyan ✦ marks a zero (with streak count), ● a blind zero. Tap any round to edit it.

Sample game in progress. Reset it under Rules.

House rules

Base scoring follows the official rules: an exact bid scores 20 + 10 per trick, a miss costs 10 per trick off, and there are 60 ÷ players rounds. The zero streak, blind zero and final-round settings are house rules I guessed at. Change any number and every score recalculates.
